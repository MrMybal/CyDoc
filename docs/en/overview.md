CyDoc helps you create illustrated manuals that people can read alongside your application. The exported reader brings together your pages, search and local media.

## Choose your next step {#start}

- **Create a first manual:** follow the [guided tutorial](doc:quickstart).
- **Explain a task:** learn how to [write and organize a page](doc:writing).
- **Solve a problem:** start with the symptom in [troubleshooting](doc:troubleshooting).
- **Understand the product:** read about [how CyDoc works](doc:architecture).

## Illustrate an explanation {#illustrate}

Use [images and videos](doc:media) to show an action, or a [short diagram](doc:diagrams) to explain a flow. Add captions and describe the result your reader should recognize.

## Choose how to write {#authoring}

[CyDoc Studio](doc:studio#start) provides visual and Markdown editing, a complete example project and local account-free use. You can also maintain Markdown files in your own project. Published manuals keep working independently of the editor.
